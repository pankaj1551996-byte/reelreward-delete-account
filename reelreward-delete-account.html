ReelReward Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your ReelReward account and request deletion of your associated personal data.

Delete your account

Open the ReelReward app and go to Profile / Account Settings → Delete Account, then confirm the deletion.

Can't access the app?

If you cannot access your account, please use the support contact provided on the ReelReward Google Play Store listing to request account deletion.

Some information may be retained where required by law, fraud prevention, security, or legitimate business requirements.
