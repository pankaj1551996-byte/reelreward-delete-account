ReelReward Account Deletion

This page explains how ReelReward users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the ReelReward app.
  2. Go to your Profile or Account section.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the account deletion request.

Can't access the app?

If you cannot access your ReelReward account, you can request account deletion by contacting us.

Please include the email address or phone number associated with your ReelReward account.

Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, your account and associated personal data will be deleted, except for information that we are legally required to retain.

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
